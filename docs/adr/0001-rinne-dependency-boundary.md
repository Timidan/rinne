# Rinne dependency boundary

Rinne treats **Surf** as the source of raw market **Payloads** that normalize into the **Signals** behind swap checks, and **Nansen** as the source of address risk **labels** behind approval and lending checks. Neither provider sits on the critical block path: the dangerous cases are caught by deterministic rule math (a swap with no `minReceived`, an unlimited approval, a borrow landing below the 1.25 health-factor floor) that never depends on a provider being reachable. A provider that fails is recorded as an error and pushes the decision to `review` — never silently downgraded to canned data. This keeps Surf and Nansen as live evidence that *sharpens* a decision without making either one demo-critical or letting a provider outage block the guard.
